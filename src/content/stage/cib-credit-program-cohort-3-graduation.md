On Tuesday 18 November 2025, CIB celebrated the graduation of the third cohort of the East Africa Banking Credit Program, a milestone in its commitment to empowering the next generation of financial professionals in Kenya.

The programme was launched as part of a strategic partnership between CIB Egypt and the Kenya Bankers Association (KBA), through a Memorandum of Understanding signed in early 2025. It is designed to support the development of Kenyan youth and strengthen the region's banking sector.
