The Great Pivot brought together stories, conversations and lessons on entrepreneurship, sustainability, innovation and turning challenges into opportunities.

Two of the panellists give a sense of the range. Neema Kinoti turned a personal need into a purpose-driven fashion brand. Eng. James Kitavi works on turning waste into wealth. Their stories pointed to the same idea: great ideas often begin with a problem, and impact comes from daring to do things differently.
