RYLA EXCEL is the national bootcamp of RYLA Kenya, which runs across Kenya, Uganda, Ethiopia and South Sudan.

## Reach and attendance

- More than 2,000 young people took part in the regional programme.
- 115 were selected for the national bootcamp in the Maasai Mara. With two Rotarians and three representatives of the programme support partner, more than 120 people attended the four-day residential programme.
- Six regional pre-RYLA activations ran before the bootcamp.
- Participant satisfaction was recorded at 92%.

## Funding

The programme budget was KES 3.597M. Support of KES 450,000 came from four contributors:

- DelloChem Africa: KES 150,000
- AAR Insurance Kenya: KES 150,000
- UNICAF: KES 100,000, plus 80% scholarship opportunities
- Rotary Club of Jabali: KES 50,000

## Communications

As RYLA Kenya's Public Relations & Communications Director in 2022–23, organic reach for RYLA Kenya grew by 60%.
