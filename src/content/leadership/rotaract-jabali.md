David has led Rotaract Club of Jabali since January 2025. The club was chartered on 30 September 2025.

## What has been built

- A new member orientation deck.
- A three-year strategic plan for 2026 to 2029.
- A professional leadership development annual plan themed “Innovation for Everyday Impact”.
- A training programme of more than ten fully attended sessions.

## Partnerships

The club has a multi-year partnership with Kambui School for the Deaf, spanning infrastructure, mentorship and an ethical storytelling framework.
